REDSTONE EX MACHINA v0.1.3 hotkeys

"H+C" means: hold H, then press C. The first key names the mod: H MiniHUD, M Litematica, X Tweakeroo, N TweakerMore, I Item Scroller, J Technical Utilities. Other keys can be searched in Options → Controls. In game: pause menu → Press here for hotkeys.

F7Show or hide the multimeter timeline

, / .Step the multimeter timeline back / forward
Left Alt+ScrollScroll the multimeter timeline
